Privacy Policy

Your documents, your privacy, and the information used to support your reading.

Last updated 1 October 2026 · Effective 2026-10-01

The essentials

  • Your PDFs, scans, notes and reading progress stay in local app storage. We do not upload them to our servers.
  • No app account is required. The app has no ads or cross-app advertising tracking.
  • Purchases use the store and RevenueCat, which process purchase records, identifiers and purchase-screen interactions.
  • Contacting support shares the information you send. Visiting the hosted website shares request information with GitHub.

1. Who we are

PDF Scrollpacer is operated by PG Software Labs (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This Privacy Policy covers the Android, iPhone and iPad app, our support service and this website. Features can vary by platform and app version. For privacy questions, email dev.pgsoftwarelabs@gmail.com.

2. Documents and reading data

The app accesses files you select through your device’s file picker. Android keeps a reference to the selected PDF. On iPhone and iPad, a validated PDF is copied into private app storage. A cloud file provider may first download your selected file under its own privacy policy.

These items are processed or stored locally to provide reading features:

  • PDF content, titles, source locations, metadata, folders and selected cover images.
  • Reading positions, settings, scrolling pace, goals, progress and onboarding choices.
  • Bookmarks, highlights, notes and generated page previews.
  • Extracted or recognized text and indexes used for search and reading.
  • Captured scan pages, unfinished scan drafts and PDFs created from scans.
  • Voice choices, speech rate, pronunciation preferences and playback position.

We do not receive your document content, annotations or reading history through the app, and do not operate a cloud document library or synchronization service. Choosing Copy places selected text on the system clipboard; your device’s clipboard and cross-device settings then apply.

3. Camera, recognition and read-aloud

Camera access is requested when you choose to scan pages. You can deny or revoke it in device settings; reading existing PDFs does not require it. Captured pages and text recognition are processed on device. Scan drafts remain for recovery until saved or discarded. Saving creates a local PDF and removes the draft image/export copies after successful completion; a small record can remain to prevent duplicate saves.

Read-aloud uses eligible installed voices through the operating system. iPhone and iPad use Apple voices; Android uses the selected installed text-to-speech engine and voices that report offline support. The app does not record your voice, request microphone access or send text to an app-operated cloud speech service. Third-party Android speech engines and system voice downloads have their own privacy practices.

During playback, document titles, page information and artwork may appear in system media controls, notifications or on the lock screen. Manage lock-screen visibility in device settings. File-provider downloads, voice installation and purchase verification may require internet access even when reading and speech run locally.

4. Purchases and purchase-screen reporting

Where purchases are available, Apple or Google Play handles payment and RevenueCat helps validate purchases, restore access and manage subscriptions. Billing requests can include an automatically generated app-user identifier, receipt/transaction or purchase-token information, products, subscription status and store/app/device context, such as versions and language. On iOS, RevenueCat also receives Apple’s identifier for vendor (IDFV) when available. Network services can process the connection’s IP address.

RevenueCat purchase screens report impressions, dismissals and purchase interactions. This information supports paid access, fraud prevention and purchase-screen performance reporting. An anonymous app-user identifier does not mean that no data leaves the device.

We do not send RevenueCat your PDFs, document titles, annotations or reading history, and we do not receive your payment-card details. We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-app advertising tracking. We do not add a separate advertising or general reading-activity analytics service.

When a provider processes personal information on our behalf, we require protection consistent with this policy and applicable data-protection requirements, and use limited to the relevant service purposes.

See Apple’s privacy policy, Google’s privacy policy and RevenueCat’s privacy policy. Contact us for help with billing-data requests. Removing local app data does not cancel a subscription or automatically delete store or RevenueCat records.

5. Email support

When you email dev.pgsoftwarelabs@gmail.com, we receive your email address, sender name, message and chosen attachments. We use them to respond, investigate the issue and maintain relevant support records. Your email provider and our provider, Gmail, process delivery and storage.

Send only what is needed to explain the issue. Avoid passwords, identity documents and sensitive PDFs; a description and redacted screenshot are usually enough. Support information is not sold or used for advertising. Relevant information may be disclosed where legally required or reasonably necessary to protect legal rights and security.

6. Website hosting

These policy and support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit the hosted site, GitHub receives the request and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security, even if they are not signed in. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

The pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising pixels, remote embedded media, contact forms or cookies set by us. External links and email open services with their own privacy practices. Your PDFs and app library are not sent to this website.

7. Storage, retention and deletion

Local information remains until removed by an available app control, clearing app storage on Android or deleting the app. This can remove reading records, notes, scans, indexes and app-owned files. Offloading an iOS app can preserve documents and data; it differs from deleting it. Temporary caches may be removed sooner by the operating system.

Original provider files and independent copies or backups remain separate. Android disables standard app backup, though manufacturers and file providers may have separate transfer or backup behavior. Durable iOS imports, reading records and recoverable scan drafts are eligible for normal device backup; temporary import staging is excluded. Manage backups through your device or provider. Keep independent copies of important documents and notes.

The iOS purchase-verification cache uses device-only Keychain storage without app-enabled iCloud synchronization. It may remain after app deletion. Store purchase history and trial eligibility are separate; reinstalling does not reset them.

We retain support messages for as long as needed to resolve requests, maintain relevant support history, address disputes and meet legal obligations. Billing and hosting records follow the providers’ applicable retention practices and legal obligations. Contact us to request deletion of information we hold; we will explain any information that must be retained. We cannot remotely access or erase a library held only on your device. There is no app account to delete.

8. Your choices and rights

You choose files to import, whether to grant camera permission, whether to purchase and what to send to support. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of personal information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent where processing relies on it, or complain to a data-protection authority.

Email dev.pgsoftwarelabs@gmail.com to make a request. We may need proportionate information to verify it and will respond as required by applicable law. Withdrawing consent does not affect earlier lawful processing.

Where legal-basis rules apply, we use purchase information to provide requested paid features, support correspondence to respond to your requests, and necessary records to meet legal obligations. Security and purchase-screen reporting support our legitimate interests in operating the service, subject to your rights. We obtain consent where required. Providers may process information outside your country; their privacy notices describe processing locations and transfer safeguards. Applicable data-protection requirements continue to apply to information we handle.

9. Security and children’s information

The app uses operating-system storage protections. On iOS, durable files are protected until the first device unlock after restart. Protect your device and backups with appropriate security settings. No storage or transmission method is guaranteed completely secure.

The app is a general-purpose document reader. If you believe a child sent personal information to support without authorization required by law, contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate. Children’s purchases remain subject to store family and approval controls.

10. Changes and contact

We will update this policy and its date when practices change, and provide any notice or consent process required by law for material changes. Contact PG Software Labs at dev.pgsoftwarelabs@gmail.com with questions.
